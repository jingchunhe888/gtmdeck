05 / Data Analysis

Gathering the data

Define variables, first
Work period

One completed span of active ownership on a contact: the trust unit. Never a period still in progress.

Connect rate: P(connect)
  • connect_dial ≤ X → success, sequence terminates
  • connect_dial > X (late) → qualified failure at current ordinal, then censor
  • no connect, dials ≥ X → qualified failure, sequence continues
  • no connect, dials < X → uninformative, skip, sequence continues
Meeting rate

Qualified meetings per work period, counted once even across multiple touches.

Qualified meeting

A meeting that clears the bar to count: not just any calendar hold.

Inner / outer loop

Inner: chase dials after the first connect. Outer: the ranked cadence of first-touch dials.

Ship a rate if
Sample is large enough, and confidence intervals between cells don't overlap
and
It changes a prioritization decision: who I'm wasting time on

08 / Ranker

How many dials is it worth

P(connect | cap, streak) × P(meeting | connect) = EV_enroll

Illustrative: each segment has its own dial-by-dial curve. A cell keeps dialing only while the next dial is still worth more than the floor; where it crosses is where the cap snaps to 4, 6, or 10.

12 / Sequencer

Right before enroll, and what gets written back

ZoomInfo verification

Confirms the contact still works there, and backfills email or phone if either is missing.

Still there: continues to Nooks enroll
Left the company: skipped, same as No Longer with Company; moves to the next person on the account
Account Notes
S1 Stage 1 claim : "Yes FS, QBO:yes, B:yes, QuickBooks"
S1:CLO one-line account research : from the Opportunity's loss reason, toolstack, close date, contact roles; closed-lost/churned only, written once per account
OPP first names on the last closed deal : "OPP:Laura, Mark"
S2 Stage 2 already ran : "09/2026 S2:Yes" or "S2:None"
Contact Notes
S2 this person's verdict : "S2:Yes" (approve) or "S2:No" (drop)
POC person of contact : "POC:Yes" + Not Interested ends the account; "POC:No" on Wrong Contact/NLWC
GU give-up : "GU:Yes" releases the hold, skips this person
Personalization
Lead source at convert : "Lead:Google Ads / Cancelled"
FU warm follow-up reason, ≤80 chars : "FU:Warm MM/YYYY (bad timing, checking back after renewal)"

Every line lives inside a [GTM] block the app owns: it never edits human text, and a rep's own notes stay exactly where they left them.

16 / Reporting

Connect & meeting rate, redefined

Connect rate and meeting rate now count one connect per account (not per touch): connect rate drops accordingly and meeting rate rises, since the same meetings now divide by a smaller, cleaner connect count. Qualify rate and avg points are unchanged by this redefinition. Dials sit on their own scale so they stay readable next to the rate panels without dwarfing them.

17 / Reporting

Connect & meeting rate, original definition

The prior definition: connect rate counts every connect touch (an account dialed 3 times and connected twice counts 2, not 1); meeting rate divides by that same event count. Compare against the redefined version on the previous slide: connect rate reads higher here, meeting rate reads lower, for the same underlying activity.
